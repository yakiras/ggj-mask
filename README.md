## I Swear I Belong Here

**I Swear I Belong Here** is a side-scrolling puzzle game where the character moves automatically through a city of shifting social spaces.

Instead of controlling movement, players switch between identities — **Dancer**, **Thief**, and **Gangster** — reacting to situations as they unfold in real time.

Every choice reshapes the environment, turning small social mismatches into escalating chaos and leading to **multiple possible endings**.
